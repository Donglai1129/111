A quiet studio for a controlled change.

MOC Hair Studio was founded in 2017 in Southern California. Over the years, the studio has remained dedicated to combining professional technique, fashion driven aesthetics, and high quality service.


Founder Allen Wang graduated from VIDAL SASSOON Academy and Pivot Point International Academy, with more than 30 years of experience in the hair industry.


MOC Hair Studio is especially known for high end color work, including balayage, layered coloring, western highlight techniques, and customized color design.


At MOC, hair is more than styling. It is an expression of personal identity, confidence, and lifestyle.
