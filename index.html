A quiet studio for a controlled change.

MOC Hair Studio was founded in 2017 in Southern California and has built a strong reputation through professional technique, fashion focused aesthetics, and high quality service.


Founder Allen Wang graduated from VIDAL SASSOON Academy and Pivot Point International Academy, with more than 30 years of experience in the hair industry.


The studio is especially known for high end color work including balayage, layered coloring, western highlight techniques, and personalized color design.


At MOC, hair is not only styling. It is a reflection of personal identity, confidence, and lifestyle.
